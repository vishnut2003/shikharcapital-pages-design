## 9. The eligibility checker — one decision needed

The 5-question checker works. It scores four signals (revenue band, profitable years, net worth,
years in operation) and shows one of three results:

- **IPO-ready** — all four met → invites a readiness call
- **Nearly ready** — three of four met → names the gap, invites a call
- **Not yet** — fewer than three → points to the guides, no sales call

**We need your decision on one rule:** at the moment a company with **revenue below ₹25 Cr**
can still score "nearly ready" if its profit, net worth and track record are all strong. Should
a company that small always get "not yet" instead? Your call — it changes who ends up on your
call list.

Please also confirm the three result messages read the way you would say them. They are on the
eligibility page; click through the checker to see all three.
