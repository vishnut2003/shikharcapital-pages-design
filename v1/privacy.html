Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 22 September 2026 · Sample text for legal review

Who we are

Shikhar Capital (“we”, “us”) is an SME IPO advisory firm with its office at 1204, Peninsula Business Park, Lower Parel, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400013. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website. Questions about it can go to hello@shikharcapital.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, company name, city, mobile or WhatsApp number and email address when you use the eligibility checker, request a readiness call or ask us to notify you about our guides.
  • Eligibility checker answers. The revenue band, profitability, net worth, years in operation and reason for listing you select, together with the result we show you.
  • Conversations. Notes from calls and messages you exchange with us on WhatsApp, email or phone. We tell you at the start of a call if it is being recorded.
  • Technical information. Pages viewed, referring source, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, collected through analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To give you your eligibility result and the next steps that follow from it.
  • To call or message you back about a readiness call you asked for.
  • To prepare a readiness assessment and, if you appoint us, to run your mandate.
  • To send you the guides or updates you asked for. You can stop these at any time.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns bring in promoters we can actually help.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal effects for you. The eligibility result is indicative and is always followed by a conversation with a person.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure how the site is used, and advertising tags from Google Ads, Meta and LinkedIn to measure which campaigns lead to enquiries. These set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but our measurement will be incomplete.

Who we share it with

  • Our service providers — the CRM, email, WhatsApp Business and scheduling tools we use to run the firm, under contract and only for those purposes.
  • Intermediaries on your mandate — merchant bankers, legal counsel, auditors, registrars and others, only after you appoint us and only to the extent needed for your transaction.
  • Regulators, exchanges or courts where we are required by law to disclose information.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and checker data is retained for three years from your last contact with us so that we can pick up the conversation where it stopped. Mandate records are retained for the period required by law and by the exchanges. You can ask us to delete earlier.

Keeping it safe

Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it. Our site is served over HTTPS and our tools are accessed through individual accounts. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, or withdraw a consent you previously gave. Write to hello@shikharcapital.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is meant for business owners and their advisers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
