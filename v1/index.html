Eligibility norms reference SEBI (ICDR) Regulations and exchange rules as amended.

An SME IPO lists your company on NSE Emerge or BSE SME, platforms built for companies with post-issue paid-up capital up to ₹25 Cr. Approval is exchange-led rather than through SEBI observations, compliance is lighter (half-yearly results), and you can migrate to the mainboard later.

Broadly: 3+ years of operations, positive net worth, operating profit in at least 2 of the last 3 financial years, and post-issue paid-up capital of ₹25 Cr or less. We work best with ₹70–250 Cr revenue companies. Our 60-second checker gives you a first read.

It depends on how quickly restated financials, due diligence and exchange approvals move. You get a dated, step-by-step plan after the readiness assessment, and we front-load the two steps that usually cause delays: restated financials and exchange queries.

Total costs typically fall in a band of 7–10% of the issue size, spread across the merchant banker, legal counsel, auditor, RTA, exchange and ROC fees, market making and marketing. Our cost page breaks each head down with ranges.
