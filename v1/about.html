How we work

Four steps before anything is signed

No mandate, no commitment until you have seen the assessment and the consolidated estimate.

  1. 01

    Readiness call

    Twenty minutes on the phone. Where you stand on eligibility, a realistic cost band for your issue size, and whether a listing makes sense now or in 12–18 months.

  2. 02

    Readiness assessment

    We review financials, structure and statutory records against the exchange norms, and write down every gap with what it takes to close it. You keep the report either way.

  3. 03

    Mandate and consolidated plan

    Platform recommendation, intermediary shortlist, one consolidated cost estimate and a dated plan with a named owner per milestone.

  4. 04

    Execution to listing day — and after

    One weekly status covering every intermediary. We stay through the market-making period, investor relations and the compliance calendar.
